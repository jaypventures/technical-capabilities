# Publication and language standard

This portfolio presents anonymized engineering casework, scoped historical verification results, and separately authored public code.

**Publish:** the engineering problem, decision, observed behavior, relevant test coverage, and clear limitations.

**Exclude:** organization and product names from the original work; account identifiers; internal addresses; private source excerpts; credentials; deployment routes; commercial configurations; personal information; and any detail that would reveal the underlying operating environment.

**Language review:** use specific, ordinary engineering language. Prefer “the validator checked” to “an advanced validation framework was engineered.” Describe the result that was actually observed. Do not turn a passing test into a claim of production success. Do not describe a new example as a historical accomplishment. Avoid unsupported superlatives, invented metrics, and marketing copy.

**Evidence review:** source inspection supports claims about implementation. Archived records support claims about what was recorded. Current operational claims require current execution and independent readback.

If a claim cannot be supported without revealing confidential details, narrow the claim or leave it out.
